# Caste Certificate — Overview

## What is a Caste Certificate?
A Caste Certificate is an official document issued by the Revenue Department that certifies
a citizen's caste category (SC, ST, OBC, NT, SBC, VJNT) as per the Government of Maharashtra records.

## Who Issues It?
In Maharashtra, issued by: Tahsildar / District Collector / SDO (Sub-Divisional Officer)

## Purpose
Used for:
- Government job reservations
- Educational reservations (medical, engineering, law colleges)
- Scholarships and fee concessions
- Benefits under social welfare schemes
- Election reservations
- Government housing schemes

## Validity
Caste Certificate is **permanent** (valid for lifetime). However, some institutions ask for certificates
issued within the last 5 years. A Caste Validity Certificate may also be required separately.

## Fee
The fee for a Caste Certificate is ₹50 (fifty rupees).

## Processing Time
Normal SLA: **15 to 30 working days** after submission.

# Caste Certificate — Eligibility

## Who Can Apply?
Any permanent resident of Maharashtra belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC),
Scheduled Tribe (ST), OBC, NT, VJNT, or SBC communities.

## Proof of Caste
The applicant must provide documentary evidence of their caste from:
- Father's or grandfather's caste certificate
- School Leaving Certificate showing caste
- Community affidavit from a registered organization
- Village form or Gazette notification

# Caste Certificate — Required Documents

## Applicant Identity
Aadhaar Card or Voter ID (with photo)

## Caste Proof
One of the following:
- Father's caste certificate
- Grandfather's caste certificate
- School leaving certificate (SSC board) mentioning caste
- Community certificate from a recognized organization

## Address Proof
Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Ration Card, Electricity Bill

## Father's Information
**Why required:** Caste is inherited. The father's caste certificate or school documents
are used to establish hereditary caste membership.

# Caste Certificate — FAQ

## Is the caste certificate permanent?
Yes, once issued, a Caste Certificate does not expire. However, some institutions
may ask for recent certificates for verification.

## Do I need a separate Caste Validity Certificate?
For government job reservations and some college admissions, a Caste Validity Certificate
(CVC) from the Scrutiny Committee is also required along with the Caste Certificate.
This is separate from the basic Caste Certificate.

## My father is from a different state. Can I still get the certificate?
Yes, but you need to provide your father's caste certificate from his home state or
any government document from Maharashtra showing your caste.
