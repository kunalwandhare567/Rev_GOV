# Domicile Certificate — Overview

## What is a Domicile Certificate?
A Domicile Certificate (also called Residence Certificate or Niwas Praman Patra) is an official
document issued by the Revenue Department certifying that a citizen is a permanent resident
of a particular state or district.

## Who Issues It?
In Maharashtra: Tahsildar, SDO, or District Collector

## Purpose
Used for:
- College admissions under home-state quota
- State government job applications (state residency requirement)
- State scholarship schemes
- Ration card, voter ID, driving license
- Proving permanent address for various government applications

## Validity
Domicile Certificates are **permanent** (valid for lifetime).

## Fee
The fee is ₹50 (fifty rupees).

## Residency Requirement
The applicant must have been a resident of Maharashtra for at least **15 years**.

# Domicile Certificate — Required Documents

## Identity Proof
Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport

## Residence/Address Proof
One or more of the following:
- Aadhaar Card with Maharashtra address
- Voter ID with Maharashtra address
- School Leaving Certificate from a Maharashtra school
- Ration Card
- Property documents (7/12, property tax receipt)
- Electricity bill (for at least 3 consecutive years)
- Birth certificate showing Maharashtra birthplace

## Father's Information
**Why required:** If the applicant is a minor or has recently moved, the father's
domicile or long-term residence in Maharashtra can establish the family's residency.

## Residency Duration Evidence
Proof that you have lived in Maharashtra for 15+ years:
- School leaving certificates from multiple years
- Old utility bills
- Old ration card

# Domicile Certificate — FAQ

## How many years do I need to have lived in Maharashtra?
You must have lived in Maharashtra continuously for at least 15 years.

## My birthplace is Maharashtra but I lived elsewhere. Can I still apply?
Yes, if you are currently a permanent resident of Maharashtra.
Birthplace alone is not sufficient — you must also currently reside here.

## Is this the same as a Residence Certificate?
Yes, Domicile Certificate and Residence Certificate are the same document in Maharashtra.
