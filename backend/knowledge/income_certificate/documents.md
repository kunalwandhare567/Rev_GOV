# Income Certificate — Required Documents

## Why Documents Are Required
The Revenue Department requires documentary evidence to verify the information
declared in the application. Every document proves one or more fields.

---

## Applicant Name
**Why required:** Your legal name as it appears on government identity documents.
It will appear exactly as entered on the final certificate.

**Proof accepted:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, School Leaving Certificate

---

## Father's Name
**Why required:** Father's name is mandatory in all Indian revenue documents for
unique citizen identification. It appears on the final certificate and helps
distinguish between citizens with the same name in the same district.

**Proof accepted:** Aadhaar Card (shows guardian's name), Birth Certificate, School Certificate

---

## Date of Birth (DOB)
**Why required:** DOB is needed for age verification and unique identification.
Certain schemes have age restrictions (e.g., student scholarships for under-25 applicants).

**Proof accepted:** Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate (SSC marksheet)

---

## Aadhaar Card
**Why required:** Aadhaar is the primary identity and address proof accepted by the
Maharashtra Revenue Department. It provides a nationally unique 12-digit identifier
and verifies name, date of birth, and address in a single document.

**Mandatory:** Yes, for all applicants above 5 years of age.

---

## Mobile Number
**Why required:** A mobile number is needed so the department can send SMS updates
about your application status and for identity verification via OTP.

---

## Address and District
**Why required:** The certificate is issued by the Revenue Department of the district
where the applicant permanently resides. The address determines which office has
jurisdiction and issues the certificate.

**Proof accepted:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Electricity bill, Ration Card, Rent Agreement

---

## Annual Income
**Why required:** The primary purpose of this certificate is to certify your declared
annual household income. All earning members' income must be declared.

**Proof accepted:**
- For salaried employees: Salary slip or Form 16
- For self-employed: CA-certified income statement
- For farmers: 7/12 extract + crop declaration
- For daily wage workers: Self-declaration affidavit (no other proof required)
- For pensioners: Pension order

---

## Income Proof (Document)
**Why required:** Documentary evidence of declared income. This is verified against
the income you declare in the application.

---

## Occupation
**Why required:** The occupation helps the department understand the source of income
and select the appropriate verification method.

**Options:** Farmer, Salaried Employee, Self-employed/Business, Daily Wage Worker,
Pensioner/Retired, Unemployed, Student

---

## Purpose of Certificate
**Why required:** Different government schemes have different income thresholds.
Knowing the purpose helps verify you qualify for that specific scheme.

**Common purposes:** Scholarship, Fee concession, Government scheme, Court/Legal,
Ration card, Housing scheme
