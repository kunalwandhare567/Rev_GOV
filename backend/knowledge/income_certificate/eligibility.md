# Income Certificate — Eligibility

## Who Can Apply?
Any resident of Maharashtra who needs proof of their annual household income can apply.
There is no minimum or maximum income limit to apply, but the use case may differ:

## Income Categories
- **Below Poverty Line (BPL):** Annual family income less than ₹1,00,000
- **Economically Weaker Section (EWS):** Annual family income less than ₹8,00,000
- **OBC Non-Creamy Layer:** Annual family income less than ₹8,00,000
- **Low Income:** Any family with documented income below the declared threshold

## Residency Requirement
The applicant must be a **permanent resident of Maharashtra**.
They should have been residing in the state for at least 15 years.

## Who is NOT Eligible?
- Non-residents of Maharashtra (they should apply in their home state)
- Central government employees for certain state schemes (check scheme-specific rules)

## Annual Income Definition
The annual income includes:
- Salary/wages of all earning family members
- Agricultural income
- Business income
- Rental income
- Pension

It does NOT include:
- One-time gifts or inheritance
- Insurance payouts

## Application Process
The citizen must declare their annual income and provide supporting documents.
The Revenue Department may verify with local sources before issuing the certificate.
Self-declaration affidavits are accepted for daily wage workers with no formal payslips.
