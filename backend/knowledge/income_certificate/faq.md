# Income Certificate — FAQ

## What is the processing time?
The normal processing time is 7 to 15 working days after the complete application is submitted.
If documents are missing or there are mismatches, it may take longer.

## How much does it cost?
The fee for an Income Certificate is ₹50 (fifty rupees). Payment is made online
after your application is approved by the government.

## For how long is the certificate valid?
The Income Certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue.
You need to renew it annually if required for ongoing benefits.

## Can I use Aadhaar as the only identity proof?
Yes. Aadhaar is accepted as primary identity and address proof.
You will still need to upload an income proof separately.

## What if I don't have a salary slip?
Daily wage workers and informal workers can submit a self-declaration affidavit
as income proof. No formal salary slip is required.

## Does my father's income count in family income?
Yes. Annual family income includes the income of ALL earning family members —
including father, mother, spouse, and any other earning member in the household.

## What is the difference between Annual Income and Annual Family Income?
- Annual Income: Your own individual income
- Annual Family Income: Combined income of all earning members in your household

## Can I apply online?
Yes. You can apply through this portal, Aaple Sarkar portal, or Maha Seva Kendra.

## I made a mistake in my application. Can I correct it?
Yes. Before submitting, you can correct any field through the application review page.
After submitting, you may need to contact the Revenue Office.

## Why is my document rejected?
Common reasons for document rejection:
- Blurry or unreadable scan/photo
- Mismatch between declared details and document details
- Document is expired or outdated
- Wrong document type uploaded

## What happens after approval?
After the government approves your application, you will be notified to make payment.
After payment, your certificate will be generated and available for download.

## What is my tracking ID?
Your tracking ID (e.g., INC-2026-0001) is your unique application reference number.
You can use it to check status via this portal, WhatsApp, or IVR (phone).
