# Income Certificate — Overview

## What is an Income Certificate?
An Income Certificate is an official government document issued by the Revenue Department
that certifies the annual income of a citizen and their family. It is issued by the Tehsildar
or Revenue Officer under the district administration.

## Who Issues It?
In Maharashtra, the Income Certificate is issued by:
- Tahsildar (Tehsil-level)
- District Collector's office
- Maha Seva Kendra / Aaple Sarkar portal

## Purpose
The certificate is used for:
- Getting fee concessions in educational institutions
- Applying for government scholarships (OBC, EWS, SC/ST)
- Applying for government housing schemes (PMAY, SRA)
- Applying for ration card (Antyodaya, BPL)
- Getting caste-based reservations and benefits
- Proving EWS (Economically Weaker Section) status
- Legal and court purposes

## Validity
Income Certificates are typically valid for **one year** from the date of issue.
They must be renewed annually for ongoing scholarship benefits.

## Fee
The standard fee for an Income Certificate is ₹50 (fifty rupees).

## Processing Time
Normal SLA: **7 to 15 working days** after submission of complete application.
Expedited processing may be available at some Seva Kendras.

## Languages
The certificate is issued in Marathi. An English translation can be requested separately.
