# Income Certificate — Application Process

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Start Application
Tell the AI assistant: "I need an income certificate" or "आय प्रमाणपत्र चाहिए"
The AI will start your application and ask for your information.

### Step 2: Provide Personal Information
Answer questions about:
- Full name (as on Aadhaar)
- Date of birth
- Father's name and mother's name
- Gender
- Mobile number and email
- Complete address, district, taluka, village

### Step 3: Provide Income Details
Provide information about:
- Your occupation
- Your annual income
- Number of family members
- Number of earning members
- Total annual family income
- Purpose of the certificate

### Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload the required documents:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Income Proof (salary slip / Form 16 / affidavit)

The system will automatically scan your documents and verify the details.

### Step 5: Review and Submit
Review all your information in the Application Review page.
Check your Readiness Score — it should be 75 or above.
Read and accept the declaration.
Click Submit to send your application to the Revenue Department.

### Step 6: Government Review
The Revenue Department reviews your application.
This takes 7-15 working days.
You will receive a notification when there is an update.

### Step 7: Payment (After Approval)
After the government approves your application, you will be asked to pay ₹50.
Payment is made online through the portal.

### Step 8: Certificate Download
After payment, your Income Certificate is generated.
Download it as a PDF from the portal.

## After Application Submission
You can track your application status using:
- This web portal (enter tracking ID)
- WhatsApp (type your tracking ID)
- IVR/Phone (call and enter tracking ID)
