# OBC Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate — Overview

## What is OBC NCL Certificate?
The OBC Non-Creamy Layer Certificate certifies that a citizen belonging to the OBC
(Other Backward Class) category does not fall in the "Creamy Layer" — meaning their family
income is below the prescribed threshold (currently ₹8,00,000 per annum as per central govt norms).

This certificate is required separately from the basic OBC Caste Certificate.

## Who Issues It?
In Maharashtra: Tahsildar, SDO, or District Collector

## Purpose
Used for:
- OBC reservations in central government jobs (IAS, IPS, central services, PSU)
- OBC reservations in central education institutions (IIT, IIM, NIT, central universities)
- Government scholarship schemes for OBC students
- Hostel reservation under OBC quota

## Validity
OBC NCL Certificate is valid for **1 year** from the date of issue.
It must be renewed annually.

## Fee
The fee is ₹50 (fifty rupees).

---

# OBC NCL Certificate — Eligibility

## Income Threshold
Annual family income must be BELOW ₹8,00,000 (eight lakhs) from ALL sources.

## Who is in the Creamy Layer?
The following are considered "Creamy Layer" and NOT eligible for OBC NCL certificate:
- Families where BOTH parents are Group A or B government officers
- Families with annual income above ₹8,00,000
- Families in constitutional positions or equivalent

## If You are Eligible
If your annual family income is below ₹8 lakh AND you belong to an OBC community,
you can apply for this certificate.

# OBC NCL Certificate — Required Documents

## OBC Caste Proof
Your existing OBC Caste Certificate is required to prove you belong to an OBC community.

## Income Proof
All earning family members' income proof:
- Salary slips or Form 16
- Income Tax Returns (ITR)
- CA-certified income statement for self-employed
- Annual income affidavit if no formal proof

## Father's Occupation and Income
**Why required:** For OBC NCL, the occupation of BOTH parents must be examined
to check if either parent qualifies for the Creamy Layer category (Group A/B govt officer).
The occupational status is as important as income for NCL determination.

## Annual Family Income Declaration
A self-declaration of total family income from all sources.

# OBC NCL Certificate — FAQ

## What is the income limit for OBC NCL?
The current income limit is ₹8,00,000 per annum from all sources (salary + business + agriculture etc.).
This is the central government norm. State-level schemes may have different limits.

## Do I need to include my married sibling's income?
No. Only the income of members who are part of the same household and dependent
on the same family unit needs to be declared.

## My father is a government employee. Am I ineligible?
Not necessarily. You are ineligible ONLY if both parents are Class A or B (Group A/B)
government officers. If only one parent is a government employee in a lower grade,
you may still be eligible.

## How is this different from a regular Caste Certificate?
A Caste Certificate proves your caste. The OBC NCL Certificate additionally proves
that your family income is below the Creamy Layer threshold, making you eligible for
central government OBC reservations.
